About me

I understand how hardware is built and where the industry is going.

I have been an engineer for 15 years, starting out in industrial electronics and robotics. Today I lead AJProTech, a product development studio where we help founders build new hardware products: wearable devices, neurofeedback interfaces, AI-enabled hardware and smart cameras.

Along the way I have worked with 50+ startups to build their products. That taught me to pair technical competence with a founder's mindset, and to look at a product as a whole business, not only as an engineering problem.

Those years also built our network of engineers, manufacturers, investors and partners. We bring that network, our experience and the whole team to every project, and that is what moves a product from idea to production faster.

I publish technology articles with the Forbes Technology Council, mentor startups at Plug and Play, and invest in early-stage hardware companies.
